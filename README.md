# Roopendra Roopak – Academic Website

This is the academic website of **Dr. Roopendra Roopak**, built using [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and the [Wowchemy](https://wowchemy.com/) website builder for researchers. The site showcases Dr. Roopak's research, teaching, blog, and academic contributions.

🔗 Live site: [https://roopendra-roopak.github.io](https://roopendra-roopak.github.io)

---

## 👨‍🏫 About Me

**Dr. Roopendra Roopak** is an Assistant Professor of Marketing and Assistant Dean (Office of Career Services) at Jindal Global Business School, O.P. Jindal Global University. He holds a PhD in Marketing Management from IIM Kashipur. His research focuses on customer engagement in online brand communities, digital marketing, and sustainable consumer behavior.

### Key Areas of Interest:
- Customer Engagement  
- Online Brand Communities  
- Digital & Sustainable Marketing  
- Behavioral Research Methods
